About the experiment

The company is the game.

Markets already organize people around ownership. Ponspany makes that visible.

The thesis

From holder to shareholder employee.

A cap table is usually a spreadsheet nobody wants to look at. Ponspany turns it into a place: a living corporate campus where every verified holder has a visible position.

It is ambitious by design and satirical on purpose. The status is real inside the experience. The promise of profit is not.
